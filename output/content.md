WELCOME TO IRON PAN!
IRON PAN was created from a simple belief — some of the best memories begin around a hot pan and a warm loaf of bread. Inspired by the comforting flavors many of us grew up with in Vietnam and blended with the relaxed island spirit of Honolulu, we wanted to create a place that feels both familiar and exciting. Every sizzling skillet that leaves our kitchen carries more than ingredients; it carries tradition, care, and the little details that make a meal special. From freshly prepared vegetables and handcrafted flavors to the sound and aroma of food arriving at your table, we want every visit to feel personal — like sharing a meal with people who genuinely care. Whether you stop by for a quick lunch, a morning coffee, or a slow weekend gathering with family and friends, IRON PAN is our way of bringing people together through comforting food and warm hospitality. Visit us at 2334 South King St Ste 101, Honolulu, HI, and become part of our story.
